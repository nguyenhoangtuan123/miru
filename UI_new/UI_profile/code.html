Philosophy of Care

At the core of my practice is the belief that every person deserves a Digital Sanctuary—a mental space shielded from the cacophony of modern distractions. My approach blends rigorous Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) with modern Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) techniques.

I specialize in helping digital-native professionals navigate the complexities of identity, burnout, and anxiety in a hyper-connected world. By decoding behavioral patterns, we work together to build emotional resilience that transcends the therapy room.
